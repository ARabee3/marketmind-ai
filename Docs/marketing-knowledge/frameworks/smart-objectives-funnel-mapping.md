## When to use

Use this framework after `situation-diagnosis-5cs-swot` has identified an
evidence-supported bottleneck. It turns a broad owner request such as "more
sales," "more customers," "more followers," or "launch the new branch" into
one objective that can guide channels, content, budget, and measurement.

OpenStax distinguishes broad goals from specific objectives and recommends
that objectives be specific, measurable, attainable, relevant or realistic,
and time-bound. MarketMind applies that structure to one owner-reviewable
12-week planning window.

## Required inputs

Before selecting the objective, require:

- one priority audience and one customer action;
- the diagnosed bottleneck and the evidence or owner observation behind it;
- an observable baseline, or a defined first-period method for establishing
  one;
- the owner outcome that matters, separated from a platform vanity metric;
- capacity, budget mode, usable channels, and measurement access;
- for launch, a confirmed new offer, location, product, or service plus a
  date and fulfilment readiness.

If these inputs are unavailable, the output should be a gap or blocker rather
than a confident objective.

## Objective-selection checklist

Start with the owner's words, then test the meaning:

| Owner says | Do not assume | Evidence-led question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Increase sales" / "زود المبيعات" | Conversion | Is the constraint discovery, new-customer flow, completion of a buying action, repeat behaviour, or operational capacity? |
| "More customers" / "عملاء أكتر" | Acquisition | Are too few new prospects entering a working path, or are enough prospects entering but failing to buy? |
| "More followers" / "متابعين أكتر" | Awareness | Which business outcome should followers support, and is follower count the best observable proxy? |
| "Launch a branch or product" / "إطلاق فرع أو منتج" | Launch readiness | Are the date, offer, capacity, buying path, and first-customer measurement confirmed? |

Choose one canonical objective:

- **Awareness / الوعي:** the priority audience cannot reliably discover or
  recognize a ready business. Measure a relevant discovery or reach signal
  against a baseline; follower growth alone is not the business outcome.
- **Acquisition / اكتساب عملاء جدد:** a functioning business needs more new
  prospects or first-time customers entering the buying path. Define how a
  first-time customer will be recognized.
- **Conversion / التحويل:** qualified enquiries, visits, or started orders
  already exist, but too few become completed purchases or bookings. Measure
  the transition where the drop occurs.
- **Retention / الاحتفاظ:** an identifiable existing customer base buys once
  but does not repeat or advocate. Confirm a lawful, observable repeat signal
  before selecting it.
- **Launch / الإطلاق:** something genuinely new has a confirmed date and is
  ready to serve customers. Track both relevant discovery and the first paid
  action during the launch window.

Write the result as:

> For **[priority audience]**, improve **[one observable outcome]** from
> **[confirmed baseline or baseline-establishment method]** to **[supported
> target mode]** during the next 12 weeks, using **[measurement source]**,
> within **[capacity and budget constraints]**.

Use a numeric target only when supported by a confirmed baseline, an eligible
verified benchmark, or a transparent scenario. Otherwise use
`baseline_improvement` and state how the baseline will be established.

## Poor fit and stop conditions

Do not select:

- awareness when qualified demand already exists but the buying path fails;
- acquisition when operations cannot serve more customers or first-time
  customers cannot be distinguished;
- conversion when there is no meaningful traffic or intent to convert;
- retention when there is no existing customer base, lawful contact path, or
  repeat signal;
- launch when nothing is genuinely new or the date, offer, capacity, or buying
  path is unconfirmed;
- any growth objective when offer, pricing, trust, operations, or measurement
  is the earlier blocker.

Do not choose several primary objectives to avoid making a decision. Launch
is the explicit mixed-stage case; it still requires a primary launch outcome
and a small, named set of checks.

## How it feeds Strategy

The selected objective and funnel stage determine:

- which objective and channel playbooks are eligible;
- which customer action the plan should optimize;
- which content pillars support that action;
- whether paid scenarios fit the declared budget mode;
- which KPI needs a baseline and measurement method;
- which competing objectives are deferred from the 12-week plan.

Strategy should preserve the diagnosis evidence and explain why the selected
objective fits better than the most plausible alternative. The owner must be
able to approve or reject that reasoning before the plan becomes active.

## Assumptions, gaps, and blockers

- Record an **assumption** when the objective is plausible but needs a safe
  early test, such as whether low first-time purchases reflect insufficient
  acquisition or weak conversion.
- Record a **knowledge gap** when the baseline or external evidence can be
  gathered before approval.
- Record a **blocker** when the customer action, offer, fulfilment readiness,
  legal permission, or measurement path is absent.
- Never invent an Egypt-specific benchmark from global guidance. Keep this
  entry tagged `global` and retrieve separate Egypt or MENA evidence when a
  local claim is needed.
