# MarketMind AI — Demo Video Script and Recording Guide

> Submission package · Issue #243 · Owner: Merzek
> Target length: 3–5 minutes. The recording itself is a human capture step; this script follows the live demo in `04-demo-runbook.md`.

## Recording prerequisites

- [ ] Full stack running (`npm run dev:full`).
- [ ] Screen recorder ready (OBS or system capture), 16:9, 1080p.
- [ ] Microphone tested; quiet room.
- [ ] Record two short takes: one Arabic (RTL) and one English (LTR), or one take with a language switch.

## Script (timed)

| Time | Scene | Voice-over | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Hook | "Most Egyptian small businesses cannot afford a marketer. MarketMind AI provides one: an AI that plans, creates, and improves their social media, while the owner stays in control." | Home, then flip Arabic ⇄ English (RTL) |
| 0:20–0:50 | Problem and solution | "Owners lack the time, the budget, and the know-how. The system provides one guided journey: Discover, Research, Strategize, Create, Publish, Monitor, Improve, with a human approving every important step." | Journey overview / presentation slide |
| 0:50–1:20 | Discovery | "First, a short bilingual interview understands the business, one clear question at a time, with no invented facts. The owner confirms the profile before anything else." | Discovery interview / confirmed profile |
| 1:20–2:10 | Strategy | "The Strategy Agent builds a practical 12-week plan: goal, audience, platforms, tone, themes, budget, and KPIs. Every claim is cited, and assumptions and blockers are shown openly." | Strategy result + citations |
| 2:10–2:50 | Content | "From the approved strategy, it generates a week of content: Arabic and English captions, image ideas, and video scripts. Auto-generation makes drafts only; the owner approves." | Week 1 content + approve |
| 2:50–3:40 | Publish/export boundary | "Now the most important part: publishing is not an AI. It is a deterministic, approved action, offered as a real publish, a checksum export, or a clearly labeled simulation. Nothing touches a real account without explicit approval." | Approval dialog; export/simulation label |
| 3:40–4:10 | Monitor and improve | "For posts we actually published, we read Facebook Insights and, only with approval, suggest a better hook next time. Past content never changes." | Metric snapshot + proposal |
| 4:10–4:40 | Close | "One owner, one business, one guided journey. The AI does the work, and the owner keeps control. MarketMind AI." | Logo + title card |

## Capture checklist

- [ ] Arabic take with correct RTL layout (no mirrored icons or text).
- [ ] English take.
- [ ] Every simulation/export moment labeled on screen.
- [ ] No credentials or real account details visible.
- [ ] Export the final file as `marketmind-demo.mp4` (or similar) alongside this package.

## Honesty rules for the video

- Label simulated or demo-only behavior explicitly on screen and in the voice-over.
- Base every claim on the current product; do not demo a feature that is not implemented.
- Keep Arabic/English wording consistent with the UI.
