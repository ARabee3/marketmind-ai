# MarketMind AI — Demo Video Script & Recording Guide

> Submission package · Issue #243 · Owner: Merzek
> Target length: **3–5 minutes**. The actual recording is a human capture step; this script makes it a straight read-through of the live demo in `04-demo-runbook.md`.

---

## Recording prerequisites

- [ ] Full stack running (`npm run dev:full`).
- [ ] Screen recorder ready (OBS / system capture), 16:9, 1080p.
- [ ] Microphone tested; quiet room.
- [ ] Record **two short takes**: one Arabic (RTL), one English (LTR) — or one take with a language switch.

---

## Script (timed)

| Time | Scene | Script (say this) | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | **Hook** | "Most Egyptian small businesses can't afford a marketer. MarketMind AI gives them one — an AI that plans, creates, and improves their social media, while the owner stays in control." | Home, then flip Arabic ⇄ English (RTL) |
| 0:20–0:50 | **Problem → Solution** | "Owners don't have the time, the budget, or the know-how. So we built one guided journey: Discover, Research, Strategize, Create, Publish, Monitor, Improve — with a human approving every important step." | Journey overview / presentation slide |
| 0:50–1:20 | **Discovery** | "First, a short bilingual interview understands the business — one clear question at a time, no invented facts. The owner confirms the profile before anything else." | Discovery interview / confirmed profile |
| 1:20–2:10 | **Strategy** | "The Strategy Agent builds a practical 12-week plan — goal, audience, platforms, tone, themes, budget, KPIs — every claim cited, with assumptions and blockers shown openly." | Strategy result + citations |
| 2:10–2:50 | **Content** | "From the approved strategy, it generates a week of content — Arabic and English captions, image ideas, video scripts. Auto-generation makes drafts only; the owner approves." | Week 1 content + approve |
| 2:50–3:40 | **Publish/Export boundary** | "Now the most important part: publishing is not an AI. It's a safe, approved, deterministic action — a real publish, a checksum export, or a clearly-labeled simulation. Nothing touches a real account without explicit approval." | Approval dialog; export/simulation label |
| 3:40–4:10 | **Monitor → Improve** | "For posts we actually published, we read Facebook Insights and — only with approval — suggest a better hook next time. Past content never changes." | Metric snapshot + proposal |
| 4:10–4:40 | **Close** | "One owner, one business, one guided journey — AI does the work, the owner keeps control. MarketMind AI." | Logo + title card |

## Capture checklist

- [ ] Arabic take with correct RTL layout (no mirrored icons/text).
- [ ] English take.
- [ ] Every simulation/export moment **labeled on screen**.
- [ ] No credentials or real account details visible.
- [ ] Export the final file as `marketmind-demo.mp4` (or similar) alongside this package.

## Honesty rules for the video

- Label simulated/demo-only behavior explicitly on screen and in voice-over.
- Base every claim on the current product — do not demo a feature that is not implemented.
- Keep Arabic/English wording consistent with the UI.
